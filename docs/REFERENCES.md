## Perception and the heartbeat metaphor

- Wang, Q. and Spence, C. (2017). *The Role of Pitch and Tempo in Sound-Temperature Crossmodal Correspondences*. Multisensory Research, 30, 307–320. [Oxford record and manuscript](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A0b412fea-eeca-448d-9de4-f160264aa2eb); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1163/22134808-00002564). Pitch/temperature findings motivate exploration; the app's cold/slower tempo direction is an independent artistic decision and differs from the cold/faster result reported in this study.
- Spence, C. (2020). *Temperature-Based Crossmodal Correspondences: Causes and Consequences*. Multisensory Research, 33, 645–682. [Publisher PDF](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/journals/msr/33/6/article-p645_4.pdf); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1163/22134808-20191494). A review of context-dependent temperature correspondences, not a validation of PulsoTerra's thresholds or accessibility.
- Spence, C. (2011). *Crossmodal correspondences: A tutorial review*. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21264748/). General conceptual context.
- Hamilton-Fletcher and colleagues (2018). *Visual experience and cross-modal correspondences*. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29502009/). Different sensory experience matters; this work does not test temperature or certify an intuitive mapping for all blind listeners.
- [American Heart Association, All About Heart Rate](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/the-facts-about-high-blood-pressure/all-about-heart-rate-pulse) — general physiological context. No climate-to-heart-rate equation is derived from this resource. PulsoTerra's heartbeat is metaphorical.

## Disciplinary approach

The project is **multidisciplinary** in bringing clinical psychology, perception research, climate information, music, sound design, accessibility, and software together. It is **interdisciplinary** in combining them into explicit image-to-sound rules and a shared interface. Its **transdisciplinary ambition** is to make scientific exploration usable beyond specialist and sighted audiences. Participatory validation with blind communities is a future research step, rather than a completed co-design study claimed by this release.
