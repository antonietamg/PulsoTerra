# Team authorship and use of ChatGPT

## Team statement

**PulsoTerra was conceived and creatively directed by the human project team: Paris Jolie Maceda Espejel, Griselda Miranda González, Paola Sosa Zepeda, Sundury Aloany Valente Solis, and Dr. Antonieta Martínez-Guerrero, Universidad de las Américas Puebla (UDLAP).**

The team identifies the project's core ideas as its own: scanning climate images through their RGB color information, translating temperature into sound, using a heartbeat as the planet's expressive pulse, connecting the mappings to crossmodal perception, selecting musical references for environmental themes, and making the experience accessible for independent exploration. ChatGPT did not act as the project's originator, principal investigator, or autonomous creative director.

The team's instruction to document these ideas as its own is part of the development record. This statement describes authorship of the project's concept and direction; it does not claim invention of RGB color analysis, MIDI playback, climate science, crossmodal research, or other pre-existing methods.

## What the team defined and reviewed

The team supplied the purpose and constraints, chose RGB-based sonification, and specified the heartbeat, temperature-dependent pitch and tempo direction, and major/minor expression. It gave the musical and environmental references: Vivaldi for flowing water, animal calls for biodiversity, Wagner for forests, Debussy for oceans, Crumb for marine life, human voices, and a martial industrial sound for the economy. The team requested the later substitution of Food with Ice Melt from the Sun version.

It supplied the MIDI, alien videos, dated record images, and ENTRAMADO Lab branding, and directed how they should appear. It required a single fluid performance rather than a track per location, cursor exploration without holding a click, an original/reference comparison, stronger heartbeat presence, smoother water violins, and gradual tension as anomalies move away from reference.

The team corrected the branding to PulsoTerra, removed the NASA logo, requested native English alongside Spanish, and required a dyslexia font, low-vision preferences, and independent access for blind visitors. It repeatedly rejected unsatisfactory sound or interface results and redirected revisions. These feedback and acceptance decisions remained human responsibilities.

## How ChatGPT and Codex assisted

ChatGPT supported discussion of implementation alternatives and possible mappings. Codex, the coding assistant used through the ChatGPT environment, helped inspect sources, write and revise HTML/CSS/JavaScript and preparation scripts, implement palette inversion and audio scheduling, integrate supplied assets, prepare speech files, draft explanations, run checks, operate preview browsers, and deploy authorized versions.

The assistance included technical and editorial suggestions that the team could accept, reject, or modify. Implementation details such as Lab nearest-palette matching, scheduling and cancellation guards, smoothing, semantic controls, and test fixtures were developed with AI assistance. They should not be represented as wholly handwritten by the team. Likewise, being the authors of the idea does not make the team the creators of NASA images, third-party recordings, or the referenced musical works.

## Iterative working process

The collaboration followed a repeated cycle: the team articulated an idea or requirement; the assistant proposed or implemented a concrete version; the team reviewed the result; discrepancies were reported; the assistant revised and tested; the team continued refining the experience. The [development history](DEVELOPMENT_HISTORY.md) records the main stages, including abandoned directions and corrections that materially explain the present design.

Source and asset provenance were retained rather than treating generated output as self-validating. Scientific explanations distinguish evidence from artistic choices. Reported checks distinguish automated tests, real NVDA announcements, browser interaction, and subjective audio evaluation. The current audio instructions use disclosed synthetic voices rather than impersonating a team member.

## Responsibility and disclosure

The team is responsible for the project's goals, submission, interpretation, final review, and decisions about publication and reuse. AI-generated code and prose can contain errors; testing and source checking reduce but do not eliminate them. This documentation does not claim that accessibility has been certified or that all generated musical choices have been validated with blind participants.
