## Project context and contributions

PulsoTerra is associated with **ENTRAMADO Lab**. The team supplied the project concept, creative direction, scientific interests, musical references, review, and acceptance decisions. Contributions are credited collectively; the development record does not assign an unverified individual role to each student.

The team identifies the use of RGB image scanning, temperature-to-sound mappings, and the heartbeat layer as its own core ideas. Details of the iterative collaboration with ChatGPT/Codex appear in [AI_COLLABORATION.md](docs/AI_COLLABORATION.md).

NASA data producers, sample performers and recordists, software/font contributors, and cited researchers retain their own credits. Their inclusion does not make them members of the project team or imply endorsement.
